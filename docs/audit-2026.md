# Audit of the five existing exams (phase 07, 2026)

Method: each exam was solved blind (passage, script and questions first, my own answer written down, key compared afterwards).
Anything with more than one defensible answer, or whose answer could not be recovered from the text or script, was rewritten.
Exam ids, part ids and item ids were not changed. After the edits the validator (`npm run validate:exams`) passes with no
errors. Listening Parts 1-2 were also rewritten so that every person has a speaker and a voice (the old clips were one "Friends" speaker).

Changes that apply to all five exams:

- Every question, gap and statement now has an `explanation` (there were none on any choice question or gap). `GapItem.explanation`
  was added to the data model and is shown in the results and in practice feedback.
- Option positions were re-balanced with a seeded shuffle, so no letter is the key of more than about 40% of the questions
  (exam 4 had 51% A, exam 5 had 71% A, and in exam 5 Reading Parts 3 and 5 and Listening Part 4 were all A).
- Reading Part 5 now has four options per gap (Cambridge format); exams 1-3 had three.
- Each recording was rewritten or checked so that speakers have a gender and different voices (en-GB, en-US, en-AU, en-IE).
- Names that appeared in several exams were replaced; the validator now warns about repeated names and themes.
- The licence URL of CC0 images now uses https.
