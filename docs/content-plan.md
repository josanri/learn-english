# Content plan: Cambridge B1 Preliminary exams 06 to 15 (phase 08)

Assignment table for the parallel author agents. Scope: only `cambridge-pet-06` to `cambridge-pet-15`. The other boards (EOI, Trinity ISE I, Aptis B1, LanguageCert B1, Oxford Test of English B1) are deferred; they will get their own plan when phase 08 is extended.

Rule for everything below: nothing in a row may appear in any other row or in the "Already used" inventory (names, scenarios, photo subjects, writing topics, invented places). Agents must not edit any file outside their own exam JSON files, their images under `src/assets/images/exams/<examId>/`, and scratch directories. The coordinator regenerates `index.json`.

## Naming convention

- File: `src/assets/exams/b1/cambridge-pet/cambridge-pet-NN.json`, with `NN` two digits. The JSON `id` equals the file name without `.json` (`cambridge-pet-06`), `board` is `cambridge-pet`, `level` is `B1`.
- The same pattern applies to any later board: `<board>-NN` (`eoi-01`, `trinity-ise1-01`, `aptis-b1-01`, `languagecert-b1-01`, `oxford-test-b1-01`). The skill text gives `eoi-b1-01` as an example; that does not match the board id `eoi` used in `BOARD_INFO` and the real files, so use `eoi-01`.
- Part ids `reading-1` to `reading-6`, `writing-1`, `writing-2`, `listening-1` to `listening-4`, `speaking-1` to `speaking-4`. Item ids are not uniform in the existing exams (`r1-1`, `l1-1q` in 01; `r4-5-1` in 04; `l5-2-6` in 05); new exams use `r<part>-<item>` and `l<part>-<item>` style ids that are unique inside the exam and never change after publication.
- Image ids: `s<NN>-<part>-<slug>`, files under `src/assets/images/exams/cambridge-pet-NN/`.

## Per-board notes for authors

### Cambridge B1 Preliminary (`cambridge-pet`), the only board in this plan

- Reading (6 parts, 32 items): 1 `notices` 5 items, 3 options. 2 `matching` 5 people, 8 texts, each text used once. 3 `multiple-choice` 5 items, 3 options, text 140-400 words. 4 `gapped-text` 5 gaps, 6 sentences, one extra, 100-350 words. 5 `multiple-choice-cloze` 6 gaps, FOUR options, 60-250 words. 6 `open-cloze` 6 gaps, one word each (add every defensible word to `accepted`), 60-250 words.
- Writing: part 1 `email` and part 2 `choice-task` with exactly one `article` and one `story` (the story gives an exact `openingSentence`). 80-120 words each (validator warning range; the real task is about 100). `contentPoints` with lower-case keywords on every task.
- Listening (about 30 min, 25 items, each played twice): 1 `short-dialogues` 7 clips, 3 options. 2 `gist-dialogues` 6 clips, 3 options. 3 `note-completion` 6 gaps, one word, answers appear in the script and the gap prompt must not show the answer. 4 `listening-interview` 6 items, 3 options. Every speaker has name, gender and `voice.lang`; every dialogue has turns for both people.
- Speaking: `interview` (3+ questions), `photo-description` (1 photo), `collaborative-task` (4+ visuals), `discussion` (3+ questions). Photos only from Wikimedia Commons with CC0, Public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA; 3-5 `describeHints`, alt text, full credit. Prompts must say what the photo really shows.
- Quality: explanation on every question and gap, one defensible key per item, no two exam texts alike, one key letter at most 45%, titles that describe their own text, all learner-facing content in English.

### Open issues and notes for this phase

- Deferred boards: EOI, Trinity ISE I, Aptis B1, LanguageCert B1 and Oxford Test of English B1 are not planned here. Known problems to handle first when they are added: the Aptis, LanguageCert and Trinity rules in `scripts/lib/board-rules.mjs` are weak (Aptis checks only the number of parts; LanguageCert has no speaking part rules; Trinity checks 30 reading items in at least 2 parts but its documented tasks have six sub-tasks of five items, so the part split is undefined). Trinity revised ISE in 2025 and the cited specification is the Third Edition. The mediation section has a placeholder UI in the app; content must still be complete and valid. Speaking image parts exist only as `photo-description` and `collaborative-task`, so boards whose speaking uses `monologue-topic` or `role-play` (Trinity, Oxford) have no place for photos.
- `docs/exam-formats.md` says writing is "about 100 words" while the validator warns outside 80-120 words for Cambridge; both are consistent only if authors stay near 100.
- The validator warns about words of 12 or more letters that do not occur in other exams; treat the list as advice and avoid such words in B1 texts anyway.
